# 🚀 CareerForge
## 🌐 Live Preview

🚀 **Try CareerForge Online**

👉 **[Visit CareerForge →](https://career-forge-ch5r.vercel.app/)**

> **AI-powered career intelligence platform for building better resumes, finding the right roles, preparing for interviews, and understanding your career potential.**

CareerForge is a modern AI-powered career platform designed to help job seekers throughout their career journey. It combines resume tools, ATS analysis, AI-powered job recommendations, interview preparation, salary intelligence, and real-time technology & career news into a single workspace.

---

## ✨ Features

### 📄 Resume Builder
Create and manage professional resumes with an interactive resume-building experience.

- Create multiple resumes
- Edit resume information
- Live resume preview
- Import existing resumes
- Responsive resume editor
- Manage saved resumes

### 🎯 ATS Resume Checker

Analyze your resume against a specific job description.

- ATS compatibility analysis
- Resume keyword analysis
- Job-description matching
- Identify missing keywords and skills
- AI-powered improvement suggestions

### 💼 Job Match AI

Find suitable job roles based on your resume and skills.

- Analyze resume skills and experience
- Recommend suitable job roles
- Identify strengths and skill gaps
- AI-powered role recommendations

### 🎤 AI Interview Simulator

Practice realistic job interviews using AI.

**Interview Types:**
- HR Interview
- Technical Interview
- System Design Interview

**Experience Levels:**
- Fresher
- Junior
- Intermediate
- Senior

The simulator generates personalized questions based on the target role, experience level, and resume.

### 💰 Salary Intelligence

Estimate a candidate's potential market salary based on their resume.

- Resume-based salary estimation
- Skill analysis
- Experience-based evaluation
- Career recommendations
- AI-generated salary insights

> Salary results are estimates and should not be considered guaranteed compensation.

### 📰 Career & Technology News

Stay updated with the latest technology and career-related developments.

Categories include:

- All Tech
- AI & ML
- IT Jobs
- Startups
- Big Tech
- India
- Global

Users can also analyze individual articles using AI to understand their potential career and job-market impact.
